Accessibility.

We want everyone to be able to explore our services and contact our team.

Last updated October 11, 2026

Our approach

We aim to make this website accessible and use the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2 Level AA as a design and improvement goal. Formal conformance has not been independently assessed, and we do not claim that every part of the site meets every requirement.

Features available

  • Keyboard navigation, visible focus indicators, and a skip link on the main website pages.
  • Descriptive image alternatives, headings, and labeled form controls.
  • Responsive layouts for smaller screens and browser zoom.
  • Manual slideshow controls and a Pause button. Automatic slideshow movement respects reduced-motion preferences.
  • Cookie controls that can be revisited from the footer.

Known limitations

Forms, vehicle tracking, slideshow controls, and cookie preferences require JavaScript. Some content and tools are provided through external websites, whose accessibility we do not control. We continue to review the site; assistive technology and browser combinations may produce different results.

Get help or report a barrier

If you have trouble reading information, using a form, or accessing a feature, call (860) 667-6330. Our Wethersfield team is available Monday–Saturday, 9am–6pm, at 105 Silas Deane Highway, Wethersfield, CT 06109. The team can discuss services and help with an appointment request by phone or in person.

When reporting a problem, tell us which page or feature was affected and what you were trying to do. If helpful, include your browser or assistive technology. Do not send passwords, payment details, or private tracking codes in a public report.
