From CB radios to today’s mobile electronics.

From a CB radio shop to two local stores.

Action Audio started in 1983 as a CB radio store. As technology evolved, so did we. Today, our selection spans more than 50 brands, from Alpine and Rockford Fosgate to Pioneer, Diamond Audio, Kenwood, Powerbass, and MTX.

What hasn’t changed is the way we work: we listen to what you want, help you choose the right equipment, and take care with every installation.

Every ride has a story.

A fresh-off-the-lot vehicle. A well-loved daily driver. A 1957 Chevy. Whatever you bring through our doors, we’re here to take care of it with auto electronics repair, auto detailing, and auto electrical installation.
