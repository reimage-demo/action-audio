Privacy & cookies.

How we use information you share with Action Audio.

Last updated October 11, 2026

Who we are

This notice covers The Action Audio Store’s website, appointment and estimate forms, and vehicle tracking. Contact us at (860) 667-6330 or visit 105 Silas Deane Highway, Wethersfield, CT 06109.

Information you provide

Appointment requests collect your name, email, phone number, vehicle details, preferred store, service, date and time, and any notes you submit. Estimate requests collect your name, phone number, vehicle year, make and model, selected service, and project details, including your answers to service questions. You may also share your trim, budget, and timing preferences. Please avoid including payment details or sensitive personal information in notes.

How we use and share information

We use these details to respond to requests, coordinate service, provide vehicle updates, and maintain service records. Authorized staff can access customer records for these purposes. GitHub Pages hosts the public website, and Convex provides backend services and data storage. Hosting and service providers may process technical information such as IP addresses and browser or request details to operate and secure their services. Information may also be disclosed when required by law or needed to address misuse or protect legal rights.

We do not sell personal information or use website requests for targeted advertising. This website does not collect payment card information.

Storage, security, and retention

Appointment and estimate details are encrypted before storage. The published website and backend connections use HTTPS. These safeguards reduce risk, but no system can guarantee complete security. We keep information for as long as reasonably needed to handle your request, provide service, maintain necessary business records, and meet legal or security obligations.

Private vehicle tracking

Your tracking code provides access to limited appointment and service updates. Anyone with the code may view those updates, so keep it private. Tracking results do not display your contact details. Contact us if you believe a code has been shared without your permission.

Cookies and browser storage

We currently use no analytics or advertising cookies on the public website. The cookie controls save your Accept or Deny choice in local browser storage for 180 days. This essential preference record contains the choice and its expiration time, not your name or contact information. Staff sign-in uses separate browser storage to maintain an authenticated session.

Accepting optional cookies currently saves your preference only; it does not activate tracking. Denying optional cookies does not prevent you from requesting service or tracking your vehicle. You can change your choice below or through Cookie settings in the footer. Clearing browser storage resets your preference. If your browser blocks storage, we cannot remember your choice between visits.

Your choices and requests

Call (860) 667-6330 to request access to, correction of, or deletion of information you submitted, or to ask about other privacy rights available under applicable law. We may need to verify your identity before acting. Some records may need to be retained for legal or business obligations. If you disagree with our response, contact us to request a review.

External websites and children

Links to financing providers, manufacturers, maps, and social media take you to services with their own privacy practices. We do not control information you provide to those services. This website is not directed to children under 13. Contact us if you believe a child has submitted personal information.

Updates to this notice

Changes will be posted here with an updated date. Contact the store if you have questions about this notice or your information.
